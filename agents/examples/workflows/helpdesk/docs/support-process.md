# The support process

Customer requests arrive as tickets. First-line support, Noa Levi and
Omar Haddad, answers within four working hours and sorts each ticket
as billing, technical or account access. Billing tickets go to Finance
in #finance, technical ones to Engineering in #engineering, and
account access stays with Support in #support. A ticket nobody can
sort goes to the support leads in #triage.

A team that has not responded to a ticket within two days is reminded
once, on its channel. Refunds follow the refund policy: thirty days
from delivery, the item unused, the amount from the order. Anything
outside the policy, or a customer who insists, goes to Rami Ben-Ari.

The support line is covered from eight to six Central European Time;
outside those hours, tickets wait for the morning.
