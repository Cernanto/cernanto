# Expenses

Expenses are claimed in the ledger under your own name, with the
receipt attached, within thirty days of the purchase. Your manager
approves anything up to five hundred euros; Dana Swope approves above
that, and anything above five thousand euros needs her approval before
the purchase, not after.

Travel is booked through the office: trains inside Germany in second
class, flights in economy, hotels up to one hundred and forty euros a
night in Berlin and one hundred and eighty elsewhere. Meals on travel
days are covered up to forty euros a day; alcohol is not.

Claims are paid with the next salary run, on the twenty-fifth. A claim
without a receipt is paid once a year at most, up to fifty euros.
