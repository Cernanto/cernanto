# Holidays and time off

Twenty-five days a year, taken in whole or half days, booked in the
calendar and approved by your manager. Anything longer than ten days
in a row needs Dana Swope's approval as well. The office closes between
Christmas and New Year, and those days are not counted against the
twenty-five.

Public holidays follow Berlin. Sick days are not counted against the
twenty-five; after three days in a row, a note from a doctor. Unused
days carry over until the end of March and lapse after that.

Parental leave follows German law; tell Lior Katz as early as you can
so the roster can be planned.
