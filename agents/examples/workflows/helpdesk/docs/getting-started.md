# Getting started at Acme Ledgers

Welcome. Acme Ledgers is eleven people in Berlin building a ledger and
a storefront for small shops. Your first morning, Lior Katz sets up
your accounts: mail, chat, the calendar, and the ledger if you are in
finance. Your manager books three introductions in your first week:
the support line with Rami Ben-Ari, the storefront with Priya Nair,
and the ledger with Tom Okafor.

Everyone is in the office on Tuesdays and Thursdays; the rest of the
week is remote. The support line is covered from eight to six Central
European Time. Your laptop comes from the Berlin office; if you are
remote it is sent by courier, ordered two weeks ahead.

Your probation review is at three months, written by your manager and
read by Dana Swope. Access to production is never granted in the first
month.
