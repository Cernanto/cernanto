# The tools we use

Mail is the company mailbox; Finance also reads the billing mailbox,
billing@acmeledgers.example, where vendors send invoices. Chat is the
workspace: one channel per team, #support for the support line,
#triage for what nobody can sort, and #general for everyone.

The ledger holds the books, the invoices and the expense claims; the
storefront holds the shop's orders and payments. The CRM holds leads,
tickets, contacts and contracts. The calendar is shared; book a room
by inviting it.

Ask in #tools when something does not work; Priya Nair owns the
storefront, Tom Okafor the ledger, and Lior Katz everything else.
