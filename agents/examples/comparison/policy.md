# The refund policy

A customer writes about an order: a return, a refund, an exchange, or
something else.

A refund is made when the order was delivered within the last thirty
days, the customer says the item is unused, and for the amount the
order paid. It is made once, however many times the same request
arrives. The customer gets a reply that names the amount and says it
reaches the card within five working days.

Outside those terms the request is refused, and the reply names the
rule it falls outside, kindly.

An exchange, a complaint, or anything the terms do not cover goes to a
person on the support channel, with the case attached, and the reply
says a colleague will be in touch within one working day.
