# Facts

Uses:

- examples/basics/fact-check
- cernanto/decision
- cernanto/llm

Given a text, pick out its claims about the world and rewrite each as
a complete statement that stands on its own. Check the claims at the
same time, and answer the ones that are true; every claim picked out
is checked, and none is dropped.

Decisions are made with cernanto/decision.
