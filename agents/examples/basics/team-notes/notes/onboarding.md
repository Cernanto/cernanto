# Onboarding a new hire

1. Lior creates the accounts on the first morning: mail, chat, the
   calendar, and the ledger for anyone in finance.
2. The manager books the three introductions in the first week: the
   support line, the storefront, the ledger.
3. Laptops come from the Berlin office; a remote hire gets theirs by
   courier, ordered two weeks ahead.
4. The probation review is at three months, written by the manager,
   read by Dana.
5. Access to production is never granted in the first month.
