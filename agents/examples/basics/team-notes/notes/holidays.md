# Holidays and time off

Twenty-five days a year, taken in whole or half days, booked in the
calendar and approved by the manager. Anything longer than ten days
in a row needs Dana's approval as well. The office closes between
Christmas and New Year, and those days are not counted.

Public holidays follow Berlin. Sick days are not counted against the
twenty-five; after three in a row, a note from a doctor.
