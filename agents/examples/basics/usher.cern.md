# Usher

One of the examples/basics/greeting greeters. Greet the visitor formally, with
examples/basics/formal, and tell them their queue number, the next one that
examples/basics/counter hands out: "Good day, Ada. You are number 7." Each
visitor gets the next number.

Uses:

- examples/basics/formal
- examples/basics/counter

Implements:

- examples/basics/greeting
