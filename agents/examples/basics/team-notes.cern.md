# Team Notes

Given a question, answer it from the team notes and, at the same time,
name the notes that support the answer, those the answer rests on and
not every note chosen. If the notes do not contain the answer, say
so, and notes about the question that do not hold the answer are that
case. The team notes are the files in the notes folder, and
the notes that bear on the question are chosen with cernanto/decision, and the answer is written with cernanto/llm over them.
A note bears on the question when it is about what the question asks,
whether or not it holds the answer.

Uses:

- file:team-notes/notes/
- cernanto/decision
- cernanto/llm
