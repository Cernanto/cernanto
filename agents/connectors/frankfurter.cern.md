# Frankfurter

Given a currency and a date, answer how many euros one unit of it was
worth on that date, by the European Central Bank's reference rates as
Frankfurter publishes them at `https://api.frankfurter.dev/v1` (the
date in the path, the currency as the base, the euro as the symbol; a
weekend or a holiday answers the last rate before it), and the date the
rate is from. The euro itself is one on any date.
